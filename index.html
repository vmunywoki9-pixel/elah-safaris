WELCOME TO ELAH SAFARI

Kenya, experienced your way.

Elah Safari is a Nairobi-based safari and cultural tour company creating carefully planned journeys across Kenya and the wider Sub-Saharan region.

We combine wildlife, landscapes, culture and personal service to create journeys that feel authentic rather than ordinary. Whether it is your first safari, a family holiday, honeymoon, private adventure or cultural escape, we build the experience around you.
