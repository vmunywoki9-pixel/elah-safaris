Family Beach Itinerary

5 nights and 6 days in Watamu, combining beautiful beaches, marine discovery and relaxed family activities along Kenya's coast.

Your Family Coastal Escape

Enjoy a relaxed family holiday on the Kenyan coast, with time for swimming, exploring marine life, discovering Mida Creek and enjoying activities suitable for different ages. Elah Safaris can help you tailor the arrangements to your family and travel dates.

Day 1 · Arrival

Arrive in Watamu and transfer to your accommodation. Settle in, explore the surroundings and enjoy the beach or swimming pool. Keep the first day relaxed after your journey.

Day 2 · Marine Discovery

Discover Watamu Marine National Park and its remarkable coastal environment. Depending on sea conditions, enjoy an optional guided snorkelling experience and learn about coral reefs and marine life. Activities should be selected according to children's ages and swimming ability.

Day 3 · Rock Pools & Mida Creek

Explore the shoreline rock pools during suitable low tide, taking care to protect marine life. Continue to the beautiful Mida Creek area for a peaceful coastal experience. Enjoy more beach time and, if you wish, stop for a family treat at a local gelato shop.

Day 4 · Activities & Sunset

Enjoy optional padel or other recreational activities around Ocean Sports, subject to availability. The family can also spend time relaxing, with suitable children's activities or spa treatments arranged where available. In the evening, consider a sunset dhow cruise on Mida Creek, weather and tides permitting.

Day 5 · Leisure by the Sea

Enjoy a flexible day for swimming, beach games, relaxation or optional snorkelling. You can revisit Mida Creek or arrange another locally available activity. Keep the schedule flexible to suit your family's energy and interests.

Day 6 · Departure

Enjoy breakfast and a final coastal morning, depending on your departure time. Transfer to your departure point for your onward journey.
