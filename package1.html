8 Nights Conservation & Community Journey

Nine days connecting Nairobi's community initiatives, the wildlife of the Maasai Mara and the marine environments of Watamu.

The Journey

Discover a meaningful side of Kenya through community encounters, wildlife conservation and coastal environmental initiatives. Visits and activities are subject to availability, access and local schedules.

Day 1 · Nairobi

Arrive in Nairobi and settle into your accommodation. Depending on your arrival time, enjoy a relaxed introduction to the city and an evening at leisure.

Day 2 · Community & Culture

Spend time learning about community initiatives, including a possible visit to One Horizon. Explore an artisanal gallery and, subject to arrangements, enjoy a cultural performance, campfire cocktails and dinner.

Day 3 · Maasai Mara

Travel to the Maasai Mara region and settle into your safari camp. Meet your guide and, time permitting, enjoy an afternoon game drive or relax while taking in the surrounding wilderness.

Day 4 · Conservation

Explore the conservancy on guided game drives. Learn about wildlife protection, habitats and the importance of conserving Kenya's natural heritage. Guided walks may be available where permitted.

Day 5 · Wildlife & Community

Continue wildlife viewing and, where arrangements allow, learn about conservation projects such as elephant protection initiatives. Enjoy a sundowner and an evening surrounded by the sounds of the bush.

Day 6 · Watamu

Travel to Kenya's Indian Ocean coast and settle into Watamu. Spend the remainder of the day relaxing by the sea.

Day 7 · Marine Conservation

Discover Watamu's marine ecosystems and, subject to availability, learn about the work of local organisations involved in marine research, conservation and coastal education.

Day 8 · Coastal Communities

Explore local environmental initiatives, including community recycling projects where visits can be arranged. In the late afternoon, enjoy an optional sunset dhow cruise on Mida Creek, weather and tides permitting.

Day 9 · Departure

Enjoy breakfast before your transfer for your onward journey, bringing your conservation and community experience to a close.
