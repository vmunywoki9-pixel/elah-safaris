Conservation & Community Journey

A meaningful journey through Kenya's communities, wildlife conservancies and coastal ecosystems, discovering the people and partnerships helping to protect the country's natural and cultural heritage.

9 Days / 8 Nights Nairobi · Maasai Mara · Watamu Community & Conservation
Make an Enquiry Explore Itinerary
A Journey with Purpose

Discover a Different Side of Kenya

Moving from Nairobi's social enterprises and creative communities to the community-owned conservancies of the Maasai Mara and the marine conservation initiatives of the Kenyan coast, this journey offers a deeper understanding of responsible tourism in action.

Meet people and organisations working to support communities, protect wildlife habitats and safeguard coastal ecosystems. Along the way, enjoy exceptional hospitality, memorable wildlife encounters and the beauty of Kenya's landscapes.

Experiences with individual organisations are subject to confirmation, availability, access permissions and local operating schedules.

Your Experience

The Itinerary

Eight nights connecting three remarkable destinations, followed by departure on Day 9.

Day 01

Arrival in Nairobi

Nairobi, Kenya

Arrive in Nairobi and settle into Hemingways Nairobi or Hemingways Eden Residence, according to your confirmed accommodation arrangements. Begin your journey in a welcoming setting before discovering the community, creativity and social enterprise initiatives that form part of Nairobi's cultural landscape.

Depending on your arrival time, enjoy a relaxed afternoon and evening at leisure.

Day 02

Community & Culture

Nairobi, Kenya

Visit One Horizon, subject to confirmation, for an introduction to community-led initiatives supporting vulnerable women and children through education, healthcare, feeding programmes and skills development. Learn about the organisation's approach to community support and social impact.

Continue to the Artisanal Gallery at Hemingways Eden Residence, where local artists, designers and craftspeople present their work. Discover traditional techniques and contemporary Kenyan creativity through curated displays and, where arranged, hands-on workshops.

In the evening, enjoy the planned hospitality experience at Eden Residence, potentially including campfire cocktails, a Samburu cultural performance and dinner at The Deck, subject to confirmation.

Day 03

Into the Mara

Maasai Mara, Kenya

Travel to the Maasai Mara, with transport arrangements and any domestic flights confirmed before departure. Settle into Hemingways Ol Seki Mara or Hemingways River Camp Mara, depending on your selected accommodation and availability.

Meet your safari guide and begin discovering the surrounding wilderness. Depending on your arrival time, enjoy an afternoon game drive or relax at camp while taking in the sights and sounds of the African bush.

Day 04

Wildlife & Conservancies

Maasai Mara, Kenya

Explore the conservancy on personalised game drives led by experienced guides. Look for the Mara's remarkable wildlife while learning about habitat protection, wildlife corridors and the importance of conserving the wider Greater Mara ecosystem.

Where permitted and available, a guided nature walk offers another perspective on the landscape, its smaller wildlife and the relationship between people and nature.

Day 05

Conservation & Community

Maasai Mara, Kenya

Continue exploring the conservancy and learn how community-owned conservation areas can connect wildlife protection with local livelihoods. Conservancy lease payments funded through responsible tourism provide an important income stream for participating Maasai landowners.

Subject to arrangements, learn about the work of the Mara Elephant Project, whose conservation activities include elephant protection, habitat security, wildlife corridor protection and community engagement.

Complete your Mara experience with further wildlife viewing, sundowners where available, and an evening beneath the stars.

Day 06

From Savannah to Sea

Watamu, Kenya

Leave the Mara and continue to Kenya's Indian Ocean coast according to your confirmed transfer arrangements. Arrive in Watamu and settle into your coastal accommodation.

Enjoy time to unwind by the sea and discover the relaxed atmosphere of this coastal destination, known for its beaches, marine environments and surrounding natural habitats.

Day 07

Marine Conservation

Watamu, Kenya

Discover the conservation work taking place along Kenya's coast. Subject to confirmation, meet representatives of Kenya Marine Mammals Research & Conservation (KMMREC) to learn about marine mammal monitoring, dolphin and whale research, rescue programmes and community education.

Continue exploring the work of the Watamu Marine Association, which brings together local communities, conservation organisations, tourism operators and other stakeholders to support marine protection and the sustainable use of coastal resources.

Day 08

Coastal Communities

Watamu, Kenya

Explore community-led environmental initiatives, with a potential visit to EcoWorld Recycling to learn how marine plastic waste can be transformed into useful products and support opportunities for local women and young people.

Later, enjoy an optional sunset dhow cruise on Mida Creek, subject to availability, weather and tidal conditions. Take in the coastal scenery and reflect on the connection between communities, conservation and the ocean.

The dhow cruise and conservation visits should be confirmed before travel. Alternative activities may be arranged if a visit is unavailable.
Day 09

Departure

Watamu / onward journey

Enjoy breakfast before your scheduled transfer for your onward journey. Depending on your confirmed transport, this may involve a transfer to Malindi Airport, Mombasa or another agreed departure point.

Leave with a deeper understanding of Kenya's conservation story, from community initiatives in Nairobi and wildlife conservancies in the Mara to marine protection efforts along the Indian Ocean.
