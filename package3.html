Day by day

Your Honeymoon Itinerary

A suggested itinerary designed to balance discovery, romance and relaxation. Activities and accommodation can be adjusted to your preferences.

Day 1

Arrival in Nairobi

Arrive in Kenya and transfer to your selected accommodation in Nairobi. Settle in, enjoy a relaxed evening, and begin your honeymoon at your own pace.

  • Airport transfer arrangements on request
  • Time to relax after your journey
  • Optional private dinner, subject to availability
Day 2

Nairobi at Leisure

Enjoy a leisurely day exploring Nairobi or simply relaxing together. Depending on your interests, we can arrange a garden visit, art experience, private dining, or a city excursion.

  • Optional cultural or city experiences
  • Relaxation time at your accommodation
  • Optional spa or wellness experience, subject to availability
Day 3

Journey to the Maasai Mara

Travel to the Maasai Mara region and settle into your selected safari camp. Take in the open landscapes and enjoy your first taste of the wilderness.

  • Transfer or flight arrangements can be discussed
  • Camp orientation and time to unwind
  • Afternoon activity where available and included in your booking
Day 4

Wildlife Encounters

Spend time discovering the Mara's remarkable wildlife with a guided safari. Watch the landscape come alive and enjoy opportunities for photography and quiet moments together.

  • Game drives according to your camp's schedule
  • Wildlife viewing and photography
  • Evening at camp
Day 5

A Full Day in the Mara

Enjoy another day in the conservancy with opportunities to explore different areas and learn about the region's wildlife and conservation. End the day with a peaceful evening beneath the African sky.

  • Safari activities based on the selected camp
  • Optional conservation or cultural experience
  • Campfire or special dining where offered
Day 6

From Safari to Watamu

Leave the wilderness behind and travel towards Watamu on Kenya's Indian Ocean coast. Check in at your selected beach accommodation and enjoy a relaxed evening by the sea.

  • Transfer arrangements according to your route
  • Beachside relaxation
  • Optional sunset dinner
Day 7

Beachside Bliss

Enjoy a relaxed day on Watamu's beautiful coast. Spend time on the beach, enjoy the ocean views, or choose an optional wellness experience.

  • Leisure time at the beach
  • Optional spa treatment, subject to availability
  • Private dinner arrangements on request
Day 8

Discover the Indian Ocean

Explore the coast at your own pace. Depending on the season and local conditions, optional marine activities may include a boat excursion, snorkeling, or a visit to the Watamu marine area.

  • Optional marine excursion
  • Snorkeling subject to weather and sea conditions
  • Relaxed evening together
Day 9

Coastal Experiences

Take the day slowly or choose an optional local excursion. A sunset dhow sail or a visit around Mida Creek may be arranged, subject to weather, availability and your chosen operator.

  • Optional dhow excursion
  • Optional local cultural experience
  • Time for photographs and relaxation
Day 10

Your Final Full Day by the Sea

Enjoy a final day of beachside relaxation before departure. We can discuss a special dinner or another personal touch to mark your honeymoon, subject to the selected venue's availability.

  • Leisure time and optional activities
  • Special dining arrangements on request
  • Prepare for your onward journey
Day 11

Departure

Enjoy breakfast and your final moments on the coast before your arranged transfer. Elah Safaris can help coordinate onward travel based on your flight schedule.

  • Check-out and departure transfer arrangements
  • Optional extension available on request
